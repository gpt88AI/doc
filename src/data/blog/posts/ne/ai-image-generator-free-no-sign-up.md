No-sign-up free AI image generator लाई पहिले guest sandbox मान्नुहोस्। Non-sensitive prompt direction छिटो परीक्षण गर्न यो उपयोगी छ, तर privacy promise, स्थायी free access, watermark-free HD वा automatic commercial permission दिँदैन। Real faces, client files, product images, private documents, unreleased brand assets वा paid campaigns आएपछि clearer account, paid, local वा API route रोज्नुहोस्।

| Route | सुरुमा राम्रो | भरोसा गर्नुअघि जाँच | कहिले switch गर्ने |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Guest sandbox | Fictional prompt, sketch, inspiration | operator, quota, queue, download, watermark, rights | face, client file, product वा repeated use चाहिँदा |
| Logged-in free | Stable casual testing | free quota, model access, privacy, gallery, export | payment, long queue वा unclear data handling हुँदा |
| Daily quota | धेरै दिन practice | reset, rollover, model lock, upload safety | quota कम वा material real work बनेमा |
| Paid app | Design र client asset | plan, commercial terms, upload, watermark | anonymous trial वा automation चाहिँदा |
| Local route | Sensitive file र file control | hardware, license, quality, maintenance | team support, cloud quality वा logs चाहिँदा |
| API | Automation र production | billing, rate limits, data, support, model availability | केवल anonymous prompt test चाहिँदा |

## No-sign-up ले के प्रमाणित गर्दैन

No-sign-up ले account नबनाई generation सुरु गर्न सकिन्छ भन्ने मात्र देखाउँछ। Operator, storage location, public gallery, retention, training use, deletion, support वा client/commercial delivery rights यसबाट प्रमाणित हुँदैन। “Free”, “unlimited”, “private”, “latest model”, “HD”, “supports uploads” र “commercially usable” लाई अलग claims का रूपमा decode गर्नुहोस्।

Low-risk test गर्नुहोस्: fictional वा public prompt दिनुहोस्, सानो batch generate गर्नुहोस्, queue र failure behavior हेर्नुहोस्, file type, resolution, aspect ratio र watermark जाँच्नुहोस्, अनि terms, privacy policy र support page पढ्नुहोस्। Upload अघि retention, deletion र rights स्पष्ट हुनुपर्छ।

## Guest route कहिले छोड्ने

Reproducibility, clear rights, privacy, upload control, client delivery, team collaboration, automation वा failure tracking चाहिँदा anonymous route पर्याप्त हुँदैन। Real faces, product images, client files, private documents वा brand material लाई “free, no login, unlimited” headline का कारण मात्र upload नगर्नुहोस्।

### No-sign-up tool साँच्चै unlimited छ?

Visible counter नहुनु fair-use limit, queue, dynamic throttling, model lock वा paid export नहुनुको प्रमाण होइन। Current terms र actual behavior जाँच्नुहोस्।

### No-login भनेको private हो?

होइन। Account नचाहनुले data processing, saving, sharing वा training use बारे guarantee दिँदैन।
