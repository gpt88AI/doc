Grok Imagine unavailable वा quota सकिएमा “unlimited Grok” wrapper खोज्नुको सट्टा स्पष्ट contract भएको route रोज्नुहोस्। Mainland China मा पहिले Qwen Studio परीक्षण गर्नुहोस्; private assets, repeated batch र workflow control का लागि Qwen-Image + ComfyUI local route; Adobe workflow पहिले नै भए current region/account support हुँदा मात्र Firefly विकल्प मान्नुहोस्।

## Route Table

| काम | पहिले परीक्षण | “Free” को अर्थ | पहिले जाँच्ने कुरा |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chinese poster/illustration | Qwen Studio | free entry, तर fixed permanent count होइन | account generation, queue, download |
| Editing/repeated composition | Qwen Studio वा local Qwen-Image | online quota वा local compute | real people/client data |
| Private batch/workflow | Qwen-Image + ComfyUI | platform quota छैन, hardware/maintenance cost हुन्छ | license, VRAM, plugins, backups |
| Commercial Adobe work | supported account मा Firefly | limited free generations | model, beta state, rights |
| Icons/vector concepts | Recraft Free, personal use मात्र | public output, commercial use होइन | client/confidential assets |

Microsoft को current page ले China लाई Bing Image Creator availability बाट बाहिर राख्छ; OpenAI supported-country list मा पनि mainland China छैन। Shared account, VPN/region bypass, borrowed identity वा copied key ले availability प्रमाणित गर्दैन।

## Qwen Studio को Low-Risk Test

1:1 Chinese poster बनाउनुहोस्, आठभन्दा धेरै characters नराख्नुहोस्; दोस्रो round मा एउटा शब्द मात्र बदल्नुहोस्; title accuracy, date, extra text, composition stability, download र edit entry जाँच्नुहोस्। “Free to use” unlimited वा queue-free होइन; आफ्नो account को limits record गर्नुहोस्।

## Local Qwen-Image + ComfyUI

Local route ले privacy, repeatability र parameter control दिन्छ, zero cost होइन। Hardware/VRAM, model license/hash, custom-node source, network, logs र save paths जाँच्नुहोस्। Fictional sample मा seed fix गरी तीन background variants बनाउनुहोस्, workflow JSON save गर्नुहोस् र restart पछि reproduce गर्नुहोस्। Real client photo बाट सुरु नगर्नुहोस्।

## Firefly, Recraft र Wrappers

Firefly मा model, beta state, plan, date, credits र commercial terms record गर्नुहोस्। Recraft Free output public र non-commercial हो; client logo, NDA material वा final brand asset नदिनुहोस्। “Free/unlimited/no login” page मा operator, data recipient, quota, visibility, rights र deletion स्पष्ट नभए upload नगर्नुहोस्। Real faces, children, IDs, medical/financial data र client assets का लागि fictional sample प्रयोग गर्नुहोस्।

## Two-Round Decision

दुई candidates लाई उही aspect ratio, Chinese title, subjects र forbidden items दिनुहोस्। Round 1 generate, Round 2 मा एउटा variable मात्र edit। Chinese accuracy, instruction retention, current usability, rights clarity र data risk लाई 0-2 score दिनुहोस्। 7 भन्दा कम भए route बदल्नुहोस्; rights वा data risk 0 भए commercial/private use बाट हटाउनुहोस्।

## FAQ

### Mainland China का लागि राम्रो free alternative?

Ordinary Chinese image का लागि Qwen Studio; private/batch का लागि Qwen-Image + ComfyUI; supported Adobe account मा Firefly। Universal winner छैन।

### Grok वा Qwen unlimited free हो?

होइन। Current account quota, queue, model र feature state जाँच्नुहोस्।

### Local deployment free हो?

होइन; hardware, electricity, download, setup, maintenance र storage cost हुन्छ।
