“No limits” भन्ने AI image generator छान्नुअघि upload गर्ने image को risk त्यो route ले सम्हाल्न सक्छ कि सक्दैन हेर्नुहोस्। Public asset वा one-off sketch का लागि free tool परीक्षण गर्न सकिन्छ; real faces, client assets, product images, ID documents, contracts, medical वा financial screenshots का लागि operator, upload rules, commercial rights र failure support स्पष्ट हुनुपर्छ।

“No limits” लाई safety promise होइन, verify गर्नुपर्ने claim मान्नुहोस्। Operator, payment model, hidden quota, queue, file-size cap, watermark, dynamic limit, retention, training use, deletion path, output rights र support contact खोज्नुहोस्। केही कुरा अस्पष्ट भए tool लाई low-risk experiment मा मात्र राख्नुहोस्।

| Route | उपयुक्त | Upload गर्नुअघि स्पष्ट हुनुपर्ने | Stop rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free no-login tool | Public assets र one-off tests | operator, quota, upload handling, rights, support | client assets, real faces वा private files upload नगर्नुहोस् |
| Upload/image-to-image tool | Reference redraw र हल्का editing | size, queue, watermark, retention, commercial terms | retention/reuse अस्पष्ट भए रोक्नुहोस् |
| Official app | Manual creation र personal editing | account/plan limit, image-input rules, policy | यसलाई backend API वा unlimited production route नमान्नुहोस् |
| Official API | Automation, logs, retries र integration | billing, verification, rate limits, data handling | free/no-login behavior अपेक्षा नगर्नुहोस् |
| Paid subscription | Design teams र predictable benefits | plan, renewal, reference rules, commercial rights | paid benefit लाई free unlimited नभन्नुहोस् |
| Local route | Machine बाहिर नजाने files | hardware, license, quality, security maintenance | license वा maintenance अस्पष्ट भए रोक्नुहोस् |

## Upload ले risk किन बढाउँछ

Text prompt र reference image एउटै risk होइनन्। Uploaded image मा faces, background, products, trademarks, screenshots, metadata र document content हुन सक्छ। Files save हुन्छन् कि हुँदैनन्, history मा जान्छन् कि जाँदैनन्, providers सँग share हुन्छन् कि हुँदैनन्, training वा abuse control मा प्रयोग हुन्छन् कि हुँदैनन् भन्ने tool ले बताउँदैन भने real work का लागि route मूल्याङ्कन गर्न सकिँदैन।

“Free unlimited” text generator मा prompt box मात्र हुन सक्छ; यसले image-to-image वा reference upload support प्रमाणित गर्दैन। Image input, file size, retention, deletion, output rights र failure behavior स्पष्ट रूपमा हेर्नुहोस्।

## “No limits” भए पनि हुन सक्ने सीमाहरू

Daily cap नभए पनि concurrency, queue, file size, resolution, watermark, model switching, abnormal-traffic control र account risk control हुन सक्छन्। “Unlimited” भनेको zero boundary होइन; भरपर्दो route ले आफ्ना boundaries बताउँछ।

Low-risk test गर्नुहोस्: non-sensitive image लिनुहोस्, terms र privacy policy पढ्नुहोस्, एउटा सानो reference-image task चलाउनुहोस्, queue/file-size/watermark/output dimensions लेख्नुहोस्, अनि route लाई `test-only`, `manual creation`, `client projects`, `production systems` मा classify गर्नुहोस्।

## कहिले रोक्ने

Operator नदेखिए, payment model अस्पष्ट भए, upload retention थाहा नभए, commercial rights बुझिएन वा failure support नभए real people, client assets, unreleased products, private screenshots र regulated material upload नगर्नुहोस्। Download गर्न मिल्नु commercial permission को प्रमाण होइन।

### पूर्ण unlimited upload भएको AI image generator साँच्चै छ?

केही pages ले यस्तो claim गर्छन्, तर यसलाई route statement मान्नुहोस्। Operator, payment, quota, queue, file size, watermark, privacy, rights र support स्पष्ट भएपछि मात्र one-off test भन्दा अघि बढ्नुहोस्।

### No-login भनेको बढी privacy हो?

होइन। No-login ले account बिना सुरु गर्न मिल्छ भन्ने मात्र देखाउँछ; prompts, uploads, outputs, logs वा history process/save/share हुँदैन भन्ने प्रमाण होइन।
